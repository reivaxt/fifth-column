# The Fifth Column

A short serious game about caregiving. Wei Ming, a widowed father in Sengkang, cares for his mother Siew Lan after her stroke while raising Chloe and Marcus. Over four days the player juggles his whiteboard of tasks, until a fifth column, and help, arrives.

Play it in a browser. It takes about 10 to 12 minutes. Sound is recommended.

## Files
- `index.html`: the whole game (HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file)
- `*.jpg`: illustrations used in the story, interruptions and cut scenes

## Notes
- Progress is saved in the player's own browser at the start of each day.
- Music and sound effects are generated in the browser; there are no audio files.
